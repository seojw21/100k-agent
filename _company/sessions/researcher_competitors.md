## 3. Positioning to Overcome "AI Aversion" (3040 Target)
3040 English-speaking founders and executives value **authenticity, time-leveraging, and relationship building**. They fear that AI will make their content sound generic, cold, and robotic. 

To overcome this resistance, we will adopt the following communication rules:
1. **Never use technology-centric terms** like "AI", "LLMs", "prompts", or "fine-tuning" in our primary marketing copy.
2. **Focus on "Voice Leverage" and "Creative Autonomy":** The value is *cloning* the founder's unique genius so they can focus on high-level strategy without losing their personal connection to their audience.
3. **Rephrase Service Names:**
   - Instead of *AI Brand Voice Cloning* ➡️ **Executive Voice Engineering (EVE)**
   - Instead of *Prompt Guidelines* ➡️ **Digital Persona Blueprint**
   - Instead of *AI Integration* ➡️ **Creative Leverage Pipeline**

To reach our **$8,000/mo goal**, we only need **2 - 3 clients per month** utilizing this premium structure:

### 🟢 Tier 1: The Digital Persona Blueprint
*For founders who write their own content but want consistency and speed.*
- **Investment:** **$1,500 (One-time)**

  - 1:1 Deep-dive Brand Voice Interview (60 mins) to map vocabulary, tone markers, and speech patterns.

  - 3 Core Copy Templates (LinkedIn post, Newsletter, Sales email) custom-cloned to their voice.
  - *Outcome:* The client saves 10-15 hours of drafting time weekly.

### 🔵 Tier 2: The Executive Voice Cloning System (Core Offer)
*For growing companies wanting to delegate content creation to a team while keeping the founder's authentic voice.*
- **Investment:** **$3,500 (One-time setup)**
- **Deliverables:**

  - A 2-hour training session for the client's internal Writer/Designer.
  - 30 days of Slack implementation support.
  - *Outcome:* A fully operationalized writing asset that produces writing indistinguishable from the founder.

### 🟣 Tier 3: Fractional Voice Director (Enterprise Retainer)
*For high-growth executives wanting full implementation and continuous refinement of their funnel.*
- **Investment:** **$6,500 / month (Minimum 3-month commitment)**
- **Deliverables:**
