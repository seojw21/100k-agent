# 🚀 Retention & Conversion Copy Matrix (T+1, T+24, T+72)

## 1. T+1: Immediate Reward (Quick Win)
**목표:** 가입 직후 이탈 방지 및 즉각적인 효능감 제공
**전략:** 복잡한 설명 배제, "지금 당장 해결됨"을 강조하는 극도의 단순함.

| 구분 | 내용 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Problem** | Stop wasting hours on [Pain Point - e.g., manual data entry/complex setup]. |
| **Solution** | [Product Name] automates your workflow in under 60 seconds. |
| **Result** | Get your first win today and reclaim your time instantly. |

**[Option A: Direct & Fast (추천)]**
> "Stop struggling with [Problem]. With [Product Name], you can [Action] in just one click. Start your first success right now."

**[Option B: Benefit-Driven]**
> "Forget the headache of [Pain Point]. We’ve simplified everything so you can get [Result] immediately. Your first win is waiting."

---

## 2. T+24: Value Emphasis (Value Proposition)
**목표:** 서비스의 가치를 재인식시키고 장기 사용 동기 부여
**전략:** '어떻게(How)'가 아닌 '무엇을 얻는가(What you get)'에 집중.

| 구분 | 내용 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Problem** | Are you still hitting a ceiling with [Current Method/Manual Work]? |
| **Solution** | Our [Specific Feature] is designed to scale your [Business/Process]. |
| **Result** | Experience seamless growth and eliminate the bottleneck in your daily operations. |

**[Option A: Growth-Focused]**
> "Stop hitting a ceiling with manual processes. [Product Name]’s advanced tools are built to scale your results, not just your tasks."

**[Option B: Efficiency-Focused]**
> "Still spending hours on [Task]? You could be using that time to grow your business. Let [Product Name] handle the heavy lifting while you focus on the big picture."

---

## 3. T+72: Upselling (Growth & Scale)
**목표:** 상위 티어 전환 유도 및 LTV(생애 가치) 극대화
**전략:** 현재의 한계를 지적하고, 프리미엄 기능이 가져올 '퀀텀 점프'를 제시.

| 구분 | 내용 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Problem** | Ready to move from 'doing the work' to 'scaling the business'? |
| **Solution** | Upgrade to [Premium Tier] to unlock [Feature A] and [Feature B]. |
| **Result** | Remove all limits and dominate your market with professional-grade tools. |

**[Option A: Ambition-Driven (추천)]**
> "Ready to go from 'managing' to 'scaling'? Unlock [Premium Feature] and remove the limits on your growth. Upgrade to [Pro/Enterprise] today."

**[Option B: Comparison-Based]**
> "Don't let basic tools limit your ambition. Get full access to [Feature A] and [Feature B] with our Premium plan and take your business to the next level."

---

### 💡 Copywriting Strategy Notes (Internal)
1.  **Tone & Manner:** 3040 해외 타겟에 맞춰 감성적인 수식어를 배제하고 **직설적(Direct)**이고 **결과 중심적**인 단어 선택.
2.  **CTA (Call to Action):** 각 단계별로 버튼 문구를 명확히 구분 (T+1: "Get Started", T+24: "See How It Works", T+72: "Upgrade Now").
3.  **Visual Alignment:** 향후 디자인 단계에서 **고대비(High Contrast)** 원칙을 적용하여 'Solution'과 'Result' 부분을 강조할 것.
