### 1. 핵심 문구 및 CTA 최적화 (Drafts)

#### Option A: [Aggressive & Urgent] - 가장 추천 (강력한 손실 방어)
*시각적으로 강조된 $숫자(48px, #FFD700)와 결합했을 때 가장 파괴력이 큼.*
- **Headline:** STOP THE LOSS
- **Sub-headline:** Every second you wait is profit slipping away. Claim it back now.
- **CTA Button:** [STOP THE LOSS NOW]

*손실보다는 '되찾는 것'에 집중하여 긍정적 행동 유도.*

- **Sub-headline:** Stop letting your hard-earned income disappear. Start recovering today.
- **CTA Button:** [RECLAIM MY INCOME]
- **Micro-copy (below CTA):** *Instant setup to plug the leak.*

#### Option C: [Direct & Logical] - 논리적 직설 (신뢰 기반)
*군더더기 없는 사실 전달.*
- **Headline:** STOP THE LEAK
- **Sub-headline:** Your current system is losing you $[Amount]. Fix it in 3 minutes.
- **CTA Button:** [FIX MY SYSTEM NOW]
- **Micro-copy (below CTA):** *No hidden fees. Just immediate results.*
