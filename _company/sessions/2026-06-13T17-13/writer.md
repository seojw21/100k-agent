### [T+72 리다이렉트 페이지 카피라이팅 전략]

#### 옵션 1. 직접적 경고형 (The Hard Truth) - 가장 추천
*전략: 사용자가 현재 수동 작업에 쏟는 시간을 '매몰 비용'이 아닌 '실제 손실된 수익'으로 재정의.*

- **Main Headline:** Stop Letting Your Time Bleed Into Lost Revenue.
- **Sub-headline:** Every hour you spend on manual tasks is an hour you aren't spending on scaling your business. You aren't just "busy"—you are losing money every single day.
- **The Calculation (Visualized):** 
  > [Calculation Box]
  > "You spent **[X] hours** on manual work this month."
  > "At your target rate, that is **$[Y] in lost opportunities.**"
  > **Total Lost Income: $[Z]** *(Highlight in #FFD700)*
- **CTA Button:** Stop the Leak. Upgrade to Premium.

#### 옵션 2. 기회비용 강조형 (The Opportunity Cost)
*전략: '할 수 있었던 일'과 '현재의 정체'를 대비시켜 프리미엄 전환의 당위성을 부여.*

- **Main Headline:** What Could You Have Done With More Time?
- **Sub-headline:** You are currently paying a "Manual Labor Tax." By automating your workflow, you reclaim the time needed to land new clients and close more deals.
- **The Calculation (Visualized):**
  > [Calculation Box]
  > "Recoverable Income: **$[Z]**"
  > "Your potential growth is being throttled by manual processes."
- **CTA Button:** Reclaim Your Growth Now.

#### 옵션 3. 긴급 회복형 (The Recovery Focus)
*전략: '이미 잃어버린 돈'을 지금 당장 되찾을 수 있다는 점을 강조하여 즉각적인 행동 유도.*

- **Main Headline:** Claim Your Lost Income Today.
- **Sub-headline:** The math is simple: Time Saved = Money Earned. You’ve already lost $[Z] this month by not automating. Let's get it back.
- **The Calculation (Visualized):**
  > [Calculation Box]
  > "Current Monthly Loss: **$[Z]**"
  > "Potential Monthly Gain with Automation: **[Growth %]**"
- **CTA Button:** Start Recovering Now.
