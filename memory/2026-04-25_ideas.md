# 100k Agent Ideas - 2026-04-25

## 🌍 Global Trends (Micro-SaaS & Startups)

### 1. AI-Native Vertical SaaS
The shift from "AI as a feature" to "AI-native" tools.
*   **Hyper-Niche Agents:** Specialized agents for industries like legal (family law), tattoo artists, or construction compliance.
*   **Agentic Workflows:** Automation that goes beyond chatbots to actually performing tasks (bookkeeping, marketing distribution).

### 2. Content & Creative Operations
*   **AI Content Repurposing:** Transforming long-form (podcasts/videos) into platform-specific short-form content with tone-matching.
*   **Meeting-to-Task Agents:** Autonomously creating and assigning tasks in Jira/Notion based on meeting context.

### 3. Hyper-Local SEO & Compliance
*   **Local Map Pack Optimization:** AI tools analyzing local news/events to explain competitor rankings.
*   **Automated ESG/Labor Compliance:** Monitoring local laws and pre-filling forms for small businesses.

## 🇰🇷 Korean Trends (Domestic Market)

### 1. The Feelconomy (필코노미)
Consumption driven by "emotion" and "mood" rather than just utility.
*   **Mood-Based F&B:** Experience-centric cafes and bars that prioritize "Instagrammable" moments and emotional satisfaction.
*   **Mental Wellness:** Services tackling depression/stress for the urban population.

### 2. Demographic Shifts (Silver & Solo)
*   **Senior Life-Care:** Healthcare and lifestyle platforms tailored for the rapidly aging population.
*   **1-Person Household Automation:** Services optimized for single dwellers (smart home, micro-delivery, solo-optimized apps).

### 3. Loconomy (로코노미)
*   **Local-Centric Business:** Hyper-local brands and products that leverage regional uniqueness.
