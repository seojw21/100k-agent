# RateParse ICP & Exclusion List

**ICP:** US-based small freight brokers (1-10 employees) and 3PL providers requiring automated rate sheet parsing for instant quoting.

**Exclusion Targets:**
1. Large enterprise logistics corporations
2. International shipping agents without a primary focus on US domestic routes
3. Carriers or trucking companies directly
4. Software developers seeking only API access/integration
5. Non-English speaking markets (excluding specific target regions)
