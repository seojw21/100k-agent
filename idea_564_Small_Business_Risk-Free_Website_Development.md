# Small Business Risk-Free Website Development
**카테고리:** AI_자동화
**점수:** 10
**태그:** #AI_자동화 #10점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Small businesses often hesitate to hire unknown developers for website creation due to high upfront costs and the risk of unsatisfactory results. This leads to them either delaying getting a website or settling for less effective solutions. The core pain is the lack of trust and significant financial commitment without prior assurance of quality.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A platform where small businesses can request a free, basic homepage preview or wireframe for their website. After reviewing and approving the preview, they proceed with full development and payment only upon satisfaction with the final, live website. This automates the initial design and proposal phase, making it scalable.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Small businesses are clearly willing to pay for a website once their key objection (upfront risk) is removed, as demonstrated by the Reddit author's success. The market search confirmed that the "free preview, pay if satisfied" model is a workable and recognized approach in website development. Existing platforms like Webflow and WordPress staging sites, as well as freelance marketplaces, already support workflows that enable this model, indicating a viable technical path and market acceptance for de-risking website projects. This model can attract a large segment of small businesses that are currently underserved by traditional high-commitment development agencies.
