# Product Distribution & Community Engagement Platform
**카테고리:** AI_자동화
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #AI_자동화 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Even with a functional product and happy buyers, reaching enough potential customers (distribution) is extremely difficult and time-consuming, hindering growth. The process is a 'grind' requiring 'consistent presence in the communities where your buyers are.'

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A platform that helps 'builders in public' identify relevant online communities (e.g., Reddit, Discord, forums), schedule and automate personalized engagement (commenting, sharing updates), track distribution efforts, and measure their impact on product awareness and acquisition. It could include features like content suggestions tailored to community guidelines, sentiment analysis of responses, and cross-platform publishing.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Builders in public, especially those like the author struggling with distribution and having paying customers but limited reach ($26 current revenue, target $3,000), would pay for a service that automates and optimizes their distribution efforts, allowing them to focus more on product development while expanding their audience efficiently. Pricing could be tiered based on number of communities managed, engagement volume, or advanced analytics. The direct impact on revenue growth for users provides a strong monetization angle.
