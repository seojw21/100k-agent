# SecureText - Secure ephemeral sharing of sensitive text

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Users frequently share sensitive information (passwords, API keys, private notes) through insecure or overly complex channels, leading to security risks or workflow inefficiencies. Existing 'secure' platforms are often clunky or require account creation, which can be an overkill for one-off shares.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
Provide a simple, no-account web service for encrypting and sharing sensitive text via a temporary link with optional expiration. Focus on ease of use, strong encryption, and privacy.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Monetization through a freemium model. Free tier for basic sharing with limitations (e.g., shorter expiration, fewer shares per day). Premium tiers offer extended expiration, custom branding, audit logs, team management features, and higher usage limits. Target users include developers, IT professionals, small businesses, and individuals needing secure ad-hoc sharing.
