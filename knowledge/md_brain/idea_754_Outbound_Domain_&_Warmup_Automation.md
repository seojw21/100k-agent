# Outbound Domain & Warmup Automation
**카테고리:** AI_자동화
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #AI_자동화 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Outbound agencies face significant recurring manual effort and technical complexity in managing, warming, and rotating multiple email domains (SPF, DKIM, DMARC setup, 21-day warming, pattern fatigue prevention) which is crucial for deliverability but nobody wants to deal with.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A fully automated platform that procures aged domains, sets up all technical configurations (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), manages a customized warming schedule, and intelligently rotates domains/accounts to ensure consistent high deliverability and prevent pattern fatigue. It would provide a dashboard for health monitoring.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Agencies are already paying for fragmented solutions (e.g., Scaledmail for domains, Instantly for warming/sending). This integrated solution would offer higher value, reduced manual labor, and improved deliverability, justifying a premium subscription model tiered by the number of managed domains/accounts.
