# Silent Churn Detection & Value Gap Monitoring
**카테고리:** 개발자_도구
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #개발자_도구 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Silent churn and accumulated exposure (customers ghosting before canceling), leading to significant undetected MRR loss. Current churn measurement is post-mortem.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
SaaS solution to proactively detect 'Value Gaps' within 48 hours, track 'Success Velocity' (core actions), and provide a 'Black Hole Audit' dashboard for stagnant/at-risk MRR.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Prevents significant financial loss from 'silent churn' and 'accumulated exposure' by providing early detection and quantification of at-risk MRR, allowing businesses to intervene and improve retention. Directly impacts MRR by reducing silent churn.
