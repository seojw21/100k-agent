## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Lead dev wants to build custom stablecoin custody and settlement stack, leading to huge operational burden (ledgers, private keys, licenses) and diverting focus from core product. Existing 'buy' options are faster and handle banking relationships.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A comprehensive SaaS platform offering API-driven stablecoin custody, settlement, and regulatory compliance. This would include secure private key management, automated ledger reconciliation, and pre-established banking partnerships, allowing companies to integrate stablecoin functionality without building infrastructure from scratch.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Subscription model based on transaction volume, number of stablecoins supported, or number of managed wallets. Tiered pricing for different feature sets and support levels. Value proposition: significant cost savings, faster time-to-market, reduced compliance risk, and ability to focus on core product innovation.
