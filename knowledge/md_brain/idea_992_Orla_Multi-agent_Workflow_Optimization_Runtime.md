# Orla: Multi-agent Workflow Optimization Runtime

**점수:** 9

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Most agent frameworks are expensive due to KV cache waste and using "overkill" models for small tasks, leading to high inference costs and slow completion times.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A managed service/SaaS offering built around Orla that provides an optimized, cost-effective, and fast runtime for multi-agent AI workflows, abstracting away the complexities of backend optimization and memory management.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Companies and developers using AI agents face significant recurring costs for inference and require high performance. A SaaS solution built on Orla's proven cost savings (41%) and speed improvements (48%) would directly address a critical business need, enabling a subscription-based revenue model for infrastructure management and optimization. Potential for enterprise-level support and features.
