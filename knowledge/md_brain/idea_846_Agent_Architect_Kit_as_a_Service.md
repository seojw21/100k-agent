# Agent Architect Kit as a Service
**카테고리:** 기타
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #기타 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Lack of structured configuration, role definitions, memory protocols, and process documentation for multi-agent setups, leading to broken systems and duplicated effort.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A platform/framework to define, configure, and manage agent roles, access permissions, memory protocols, and deployment processes for multi-agent systems, offering templates and best practices.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Subscription model based on the number of agents, projects, or advanced features.
