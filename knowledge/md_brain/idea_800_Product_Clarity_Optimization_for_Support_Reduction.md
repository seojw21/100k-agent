# Product Clarity Optimization for Support Reduction
**카테고리:** AI_자동화
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #AI_자동화 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Overwhelmed support teams due to product confusion (unclear UI, onboarding, hidden features), leading to repetitive tickets, user frustration, and high churn.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
An AI-powered platform to analyze user behavior and support tickets, identify product clarity issues, and suggest precise UX/UI improvements (e.g., better labels, onboarding steps, in-app prompts) to proactively reduce confusion and support load.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Solves a critical and costly problem (support team burnout, high operational costs, user churn) for growing companies. Value proposition is clear ROI through reduced support costs and improved retention, justifying a tiered subscription model based on product usage or features.
