## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Website owners often discover broken features after customers complain, leading to lost business and reputational damage. Existing tools provide alerts but often lack specific details on *what* is broken and *how* to fix it, requiring manual investigation.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A SaaS platform that proactively monitors website functionality (uptime, broken links, critical user flows), identifies specific broken features, and provides actionable insights or steps to fix them. It could offer customizable alert mechanisms (email, SMS, Slack) and a dashboard for issue tracking.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
High willingness to pay for a solution that prevents customer-facing issues and reduces manual debugging time. Pricing can be tiered based on the number of websites monitored, frequency of checks, depth of analysis (e.g., simple uptime vs. complex user flows), alert channels, and the level of detail in fix suggestions. The market research shows existing tools range from $10-$200+ per month, with demand for both basic and advanced solutions. The 'how to fix it' feature adds significant value justifying premium pricing.
