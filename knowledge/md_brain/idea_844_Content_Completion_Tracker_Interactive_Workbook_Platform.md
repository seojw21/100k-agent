# Content Completion Tracker / Interactive Workbook Platform

**점수:** 9
**태그:** #개발자_도구 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Creators spend time making workbooks/guides/PDFs, but users rarely complete them due to a lack of engaging experience and tracking, leading to wasted effort and unknown effectiveness. Existing solutions like fillable PDFs are insufficient.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A platform that converts static PDFs into interactive, hosted, fillable workbooks with completion tracking and analytics (like Formely).

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Content creators would pay for a service that increases user engagement and completion rates for their digital products, providing valuable insights to improve content and demonstrate ROI.
