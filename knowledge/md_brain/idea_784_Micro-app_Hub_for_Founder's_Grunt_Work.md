# Micro-app Hub for Founder's Grunt Work
**카테고리:** 기타
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #기타 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Founders experience significant workflow disruption and loss of focus due to constantly switching between multiple, bloated software platforms for simple, single-use administrative tasks (e.g., UTM links, NDAs, resizing assets, profit margin calculations). Existing software is too heavy for these 'grunt-work' micro-tasks, leading to 'tab-shuffling' and breaking flow state.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A curated hub of frictionless, bloat-free, single-use micro-apps unbundled from heavy enterprise software, allowing founders to quickly complete administrative tasks without logging into multiple platforms or navigating complex dashboards.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
The solution addresses a common, recurring pain point for founders who are willing to use a dedicated platform to save time and maintain focus. Monetization could involve premium micro-apps, increased usage tiers, or integrations with other tools.
