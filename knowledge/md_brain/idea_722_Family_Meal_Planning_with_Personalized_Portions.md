# Family Meal Planning with Personalized Portions
**카테고리:** AI_자동화
**점수:** 9
**태그:** #AI_자동화 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Existing nutrition apps are designed for individuals, not families with diverse nutritional needs eating the same meal. This leads to daily struggles in meal planning and portioning for different family members (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, child growth). The lack of accurate Indian nutrition data is an additional pain point.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A SaaS platform that allows families to plan shared meals while automatically adjusting portion sizes and nutritional breakdowns per family member based on individual goals (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, child growth). It should incorporate a robust database for diverse cuisines, especially focusing on improving data for regional cuisines like North Indian. The platform could offer recipe suggestions, grocery lists, and even integrate with smart kitchen devices.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Subscription model (monthly/yearly) for families. Premium features could include advanced dietary filters, integration with smart home devices, personalized nutritionist consultations, or meal kit partnerships. The value proposition is significant time savings and reduced stress for household meal planners, coupled with ensuring optimal nutrition for all family members.
