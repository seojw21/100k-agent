# Freelance Scope Guard

**점수:** 9
**태그:** #AI_자동화 #9점

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Freelancers and small businesses face significant frustration and financial loss due to "scope creep," where clients request additional work beyond the agreed-upon project scope without corresponding payment or contract adjustments. This leads to unpaid labor, project delays, and strained client relationships.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A web-based platform that allows freelancers and their clients to clearly define project scopes, track changes, submit and approve change orders with associated costs and timelines, and automatically generate updated contracts or addendums. It would provide a clear, immutable record of all agreements and changes.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Freelancers: Tiered subscription model based on the number of active projects or clients. Value proposition: save time, avoid unpaid work, maintain professional relationships. Small Businesses/Agencies: Higher-tier subscription or enterprise plan for managing multiple freelance contracts and ensuring project adherence. Value proposition: clear budgeting, project control, reduced disputes. Could also offer premium features like legal template integration, automated invoicing for change orders, or integration with project management tools.
