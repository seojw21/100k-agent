# First-Week Churn Prevention System

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
SaaS companies frequently experience significant user churn (e.g., 20% or more) within the first week of signup, primarily due to 'messy' or ineffective onboarding experiences. This problem represents a massive loss of potential revenue and indicates that current onboarding strategies fail to effectively guide new users to immediate value and long-term engagement.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
Proactive First-Week Engagement & Retention Platform: A SaaS solution that monitors new user behavior in real-time during their first week, identifies early signs of disengagement or confusion, and proactively triggers targeted interventions. This could include personalized in-app messages, short instructional videos, contextual tooltips, or automated outreach from customer success, all designed to guide users to key activation milestones and prevent early churn. It would also provide detailed analytics on early-week user drop-off points and the effectiveness of interventions.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Preventing early user churn has a direct and substantial impact on SaaS revenue and customer lifetime value. Companies will invest significantly in tools that can demonstrably reduce first-week churn, as it's far more cost-effective than acquiring new customers. Monetization could be based on the number of active users monitored, the volume of automated interventions, or a tiered model offering advanced analytics, AI-driven insights, and integration with existing CRM/marketing automation tools.
