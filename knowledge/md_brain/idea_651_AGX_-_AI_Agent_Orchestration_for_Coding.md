## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Managing durable state, crash-safe resume, and checkpoints for AI coding tasks is complex and manual. Orchestrating multiple AI agents for large projects requires significant effort in coordination and management, akin to running a virtual team.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A SaaS platform providing advanced orchestration, state management, crash recovery, and checkpointing for AI coding agents. Extend this to a "virtual company" model allowing users to define departments and assign AI agents for self-organizing and collaborative project completion.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Developers and organizations leveraging AI for coding will pay for a robust platform that automates complex agent orchestration, ensures reliability, and improves productivity in managing AI development teams. Subscription tiers can be based on agent count, project size, or advanced features.
