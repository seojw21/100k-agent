## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Small business owners are tired of repetitively answering the same customer questions daily, leading to wasted time and missed lead opportunities.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A SaaS platform that enables freelancers and agencies to easily set up, train, and manage custom AI chatbots for local businesses, offering automated customer support, lead capture, and website embedding. It should provide a user-friendly interface for uploading business data, customizing prompts, and integrating with CRMs/email lists.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Freelancers and agencies pay a subscription fee for the platform to manage multiple client chatbots, potentially with tiered pricing based on the number of bots or advanced features. Local businesses pay freelancers/agencies for the setup and ongoing maintenance, creating a viable channel for SaaS revenue.
