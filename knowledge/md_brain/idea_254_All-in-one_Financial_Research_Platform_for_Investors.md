# All-in-one Financial Research Platform for Investors

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
Investors face high costs and fragmentation when aggregating financial data from multiple APIs and platforms, leading to inefficient research and analysis workflows. The manual process of combining disparate data sources is time-consuming and expensive.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
Develop a comprehensive, integrated SaaS platform that centralizes all essential financial research tools and data sources, offering a more affordable and efficient alternative to existing high-cost, fragmented solutions. Implement a freemium model with advanced features for subscribers.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Web market search results show established, expensive enterprise solutions like Bloomberg Terminal and Refinitiv Eikon, confirming a strong willingness to pay for comprehensive financial intelligence. There's a clear gap between these high-cost platforms and basic free tools for retail investors. A more affordable, user-friendly, and integrated platform with a freemium model can capture market share from individual investors, small funds, and professionals seeking cost-effective, yet robust, alternatives. Monetization can come from premium features, advanced data access, or professional subscriptions.
