# Technical founder's overwhelm in non-coding aspects: marketing, user acquisition, and feedback.

## 😟 핵심 통증 (Pain Point)
기술 기반 창업가(데이터 엔지니어)가 앱 개발 외의 마케팅, 사용자 확보, 피드백 수집, 프론트엔드, 법률 등 비기술적 업무에 큰 어려움을 겪고 있으며, 이로 인해 시간 낭비와 번아웃을 경험하고 있음. 산업 변화에 뒤처지고 핵심 역량 외의 업무 처리로 앱 성공에 대한 확신을 잃고 있음.

## 💡 해결 방안 (Solution)
A comprehensive platform or service designed for technical founders that provides guided marketing strategies, AI-powered content generation for landing pages and SEO, integrated user feedback tools, and perhaps a curated marketplace for vetted freelancers (frontend, legal, compliance, marketing specialists) specifically for early-stage SaaS.

## 💰 수익화 근거 (Monetization)
Technical founders are a huge market segment with a high willingness to pay for solutions that let them focus on product while ensuring their product reaches users. The subscription model could include tiered access to tools, expert advice, and freelancer matching services. The market offers numerous specialized SaaS solutions for various aspects of marketing, user feedback, analytics, onboarding, and support (e.g., HubSpot, Pendo, Mixpanel, Canny). However, the sheer volume and complexity of integrating these tools highlight a need for a consolidated, guided approach or an integrated platform that simplifies selection, integration, and strategic implementation for technical founders overwhelmed by non-coding demands.
